## Research questions

You may also ask the lab's library to look things up on the web. Ask only about what neither team showed it knew and what could settle the matter: how a method is defined, what a tool does by default, which convention a field uses. Return up to three in `research_questions`, each with why it matters. The lab checks each one and gives those that pass to the library; the library's findings go to both teams before they revise.

A research question must never contain either team's answer values, in numbers or in words, or anything that identifies this task's dataset (its name, its source study, an accession or a file name). Ask about the general method, not about this data. A question that breaks this rule is not asked.
